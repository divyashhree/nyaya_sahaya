I, Priya Sharma, through my advocate, hereby serve you this legal notice under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, for the following reasons:

FACTS OF THE CASE:

1. On January 15, 2025, you borrowed a sum of Rs. 5,00,000 (Five Lakh Rupees) from me for business purposes.

2. As security for the said loan, you issued a post-dated cheque bearing number 123456, dated March 1, 2025, drawn on HDFC Bank, Mumbai Branch.

3. The agreed terms were that you would repay the amount on or before March 1, 2025.

4. I deposited the cheque on March 2, 2025, but it was dishonored by the bank with the remark "Insufficient Funds".

5. Despite repeated requests and reminders, you have failed to repay the borrowed amount.

6. A legal notice was sent to you on March 15, 2025, giving you 15 days to make the payment, but you have not responded.

LEGAL GROUNDS:

This constitutes a clear violation of Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, which deals with dishonor of cheques for insufficiency of funds. As per the provisions of this section, dishonor of a cheque is a criminal offense punishable with imprisonment up to 2 years or a fine up to twice the cheque amount, or both.

DEMAND:

You are hereby called upon to pay the outstanding amount of Rs. 5,00,000 (Five Lakh Rupees) along with interest at 12% per annum from the date of default, within 15 days from the receipt of this notice.

CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE:

If you fail to make the payment within the stipulated period, I shall be constrained to initiate criminal proceedings against you under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, without any further notice. You will also be liable for all legal costs and expenses incurred in this matter.

This notice is being issued without prejudice to my other rights and remedies available under law.
