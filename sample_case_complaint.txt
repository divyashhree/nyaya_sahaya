PLAINT UNDER ORDER VII RULE 1 OF THE CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908

FACTS OF THE CASE:

1. The Plaintiff is a software development consultant and freelance developer with over 15 years of experience in the IT industry, specializing in enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems and custom software solutions.

2. The Defendant is a private limited company engaged in providing IT services, software development, and digital transformation solutions to various corporate clients across India.

3. On 1st March 2025, the Plaintiff and Defendant entered into a written Agreement for Development of Custom ERP Software ("the Agreement") wherein the Plaintiff was engaged to develop a comprehensive ERP system for the Defendant's client, ABC Manufacturing Ltd.

4. As per Clause 3 of the Agreement, the total contract value was fixed at Rs. 25,00,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Five Lakhs Only), payable in the following manner:
   a) 30% (Rs. 7,50,000/-) as advance upon signing - PAID
   b) 40% (Rs. 10,00,000/-) upon delivery of beta version - PAID
   c) 30% (Rs. 7,50,000/-) upon final delivery and acceptance - UNPAID

5. The Plaintiff diligently performed all his obligations under the Agreement and completed the development work within the stipulated timeline of 6 months, i.e., by 31st August 2025.

6. On 5th September 2025, the Plaintiff delivered the final version of the ERP software to the Defendant, complete with source code, documentation, user manuals, and training materials, as evidenced by the Delivery Acceptance Note signed by Mr. Rahul Sharma, Project Manager of the Defendant company.

7. The Defendant deployed the software for their client ABC Manufacturing Ltd. and the same has been operational since 15th September 2025, generating revenue for the Defendant.

8. Despite repeated requests, reminders, and follow-ups made on the following dates, the Defendant has willfully and deliberately failed to release the final payment of Rs. 7,50,000/-:
   - Verbal reminder on 20th September 2025
   - Email reminder on 30th September 2025
   - Formal letter dated 15th October 2025
   - Legal notice dated 5th November 2025

9. The Defendant, in response to the legal notice, vide their email dated 20th November 2025, raised baseless and frivolous objections regarding alleged "minor bugs" in the software, despite having accepted the delivery and deploying the same for commercial use.

10. The Defendant's conduct amounts to breach of contract under Section 73 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, and constitutes unjust enrichment by using the Plaintiff's intellectual property and work product while denying legitimate payment.

11. The Plaintiff has suffered mental agony, financial hardship, and reputational damage due to the Defendant's willful default and breach of contract.

CAUSE OF ACTION:

12. The cause of action arose on 5th September 2025 when the final payment became due upon delivery and acceptance of the software, and continues to arise every day the Defendant fails to make the payment.

JURISDICTION:

13. This Hon'ble Court has jurisdiction to entertain this suit as:
    a) The Defendant has a place of business within the territorial jurisdiction of this Court
    b) The cause of action arose within the jurisdiction of this Court
    c) The Agreement was executed in New Delhi

RELIEF CLAIMED:

14. In light of the above facts and circumstances, the Plaintiff most respectfully prays that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:

    a) Direct the Defendant to pay Rs. 7,50,000/- (Rupees Seven Lakhs Fifty Thousand Only) being the outstanding amount under the Agreement dated 1st March 2025;

    b) Direct the Defendant to pay interest @ 18% per annum on the said amount from 5th September 2025 till the date of actual payment;

    c) Direct the Defendant to pay Rs. 2,00,000/- (Rupees Two Lakhs Only) as compensation for mental agony and harassment;

    d) Direct the Defendant to pay costs of this suit;

    e) Grant any other relief that this Hon'ble Court may deem fit and proper in the interest of justice.

VALUATION AND COURT FEES:
